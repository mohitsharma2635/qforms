Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know.

What is QForms?

QForms is an online survey panel that connects you with brands and researchers who want to hear your opinions. You earn rewards for every survey you complete.

How does it work?

Create a free account, tell us a bit about yourself, and we'll send you surveys that match your profile. Complete them to earn points you can redeem for rewards.

Is it really free?

Yes. Joining and taking part is completely free — there are no subscriptions or hidden charges.

Who can join?

Anyone aged 16 or over with a valid email address can join.

Do I need any experience?

Not at all. Just answer honestly — there are no right or wrong answers.

How do I sign up?

Click “Join our Panel”, enter your email, confirm your account and you're ready to go.

Can I register from my phone?

Yes. The site works on any phone, tablet or computer.
